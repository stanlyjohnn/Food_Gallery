Mandhi

Chicken Mandhi is a traditional Arabian rice dish made with fragrant, spiced basmati rice and tender, slow-cooked chicken. It is usually served with salad, chutney, and a flavorful sauce.It is Tasty and delicious.

Biriyani

Biriyani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, flavorful spices, and tender chicken, mutton, beef, or vegetables. It is usually cooked with onions, herbs, and aromatic spices.It is Tasty and delicious.

Sadhya

Sadhya is a traditional Kerala vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf. It includes a variety of dishes such as rice, sambar, avial, thoran, olan, pachadi, pickles, and payasam, offering a rich combination of flavors.

Porotta

Porotta is a popular Kerala flatbread made from refined flour and layered with oil or ghee. It is crispy on the outside, soft and flaky inside, and is commonly served with chicken curry, beef curry, or vegetable curry.
